How to Trace

Click to place anchors around the region · click-drag pulls curve handles
L / Next button: next segment is a Curve (dot mark) or Line (bar mark)
Pen: pick the outline colour and width
Ctrl-click: place the other type once · Alt-click a segment to flip it
Click the green first anchor or press Enter to close
Double-click anchor: smooth / corner · Shift-click: delete it
Backspace: undo point · Esc: discard open outline
Wheel: zoom · Right-drag, middle-drag or Space+drag: pan
[ / ]: previous / next image
Follow (key F): new anchors glide to the centre and the image scrolls when the mouse nears an edge — zoom in once and trace the whole boundary
Points within 8 px of the image border snap onto it.
